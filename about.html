About Our Firm

Experienced attorneys, rooted in the Overstrand

For over two decades, Bosman Smit Pretorius Inc has provided considered, dependable legal counsel to the people and businesses of the Overstrand and the Western Cape.

LPC Registered practitioners
Firm Overview

Big-firm expertise with a personal touch

Founded on the belief that excellent legal service should be both highly skilled and genuinely personal, our firm has grown into one of the Overstrand's trusted names in law.

Our attorneys and conveyancers advise on family law, property and conveyancing, civil litigation, commercial and contract law, and estate planning. We act for private individuals, families, property developers, local businesses and established companies.

Because we live and work in the communities we serve — from Hermanus and Onrus to Kleinmond, Stanford and Gansbaai — we understand the local property market, the courts and the people. That insight translates into faster, smarter outcomes for our clients.

Our Journey

A legacy of legal excellence

  1. 2000

    The firm is founded

    Opened its doors in Hermanus with a focus on conveyancing and personal legal services for the local community.

  2. 2007

    Litigation department

    Expanded into civil litigation and family law, representing clients in the Magistrates' and High Courts.

  3. 2013

    Partnership formed

    Bosman, Smit and Pretorius join forces, combining their expertise into a full-service Overstrand practice.

  4. 2019

    Commercial & estates

    Launched dedicated commercial law and estate planning divisions to serve a growing business and retiree community.

  5. Today

    Trusted across the Overstrand

    A modern, digitally-enabled firm serving clients from Pringle Bay to Gansbaai and throughout the Western Cape.
